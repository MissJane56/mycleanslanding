1.2 We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. "Personal information" means information about an identifiable person.

## 2. What we collect

### 2.1 On the Website

- **Beta applications and waitlist.** Your name, your email, whether you're a cleaner or a host, and where you found us. "Where you found us" is the source of the link you used (for example "instagram"), or the website that sent you, or "direct" if we can't tell.

- **Technical data.** When you visit, our hosting provider records technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you request, and the times. We use it to keep the Website running and secure.
- **Fonts.** The Website loads its fonts from Google. This means your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads, and Google receives technical information such as your IP address.
- **Cookies.** We don't set cookies on the Website for tracking or advertising. [Update this if you add analytics.]

### 2.2 In the App

The App is being built and released in stages. This section describes what it's designed to collect. Some features may not be available yet.

- **Account and profile.** Your name, email, phone number, password (stored securely), whether you're a cleaner or host, and your business name and ABN if you give them. Cleaners may have a profile (such as service areas and experience) that hosts can see in our directory.
- **Property information (hosts).** Property addresses, access instructions (such as key or door-code details), notes and house rules.
- **Jobs and bookings.** Dates, times, tasks and status. If you link a booking calendar (such as from Airbnb or VRBO), we receive the calendar data it shares, which may include booking dates and [guest names or booking references].
- **Condition reports and photos.** Answers to report questions, star ratings, notes, and photos (with timestamps). Photos may accidentally show people or personal belongings.
- **Messages.** Messages between cleaners and hosts in the App.
- **Invoices and payments.** Invoice details, amounts and payment status. Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive limited information, such as payment status and the card type and last four digits. We don't store full card numbers.
- **Verification.** [If you verify hosts or cleaners, describe what you collect, for example ABN checks or other documents. Delete if not applicable.]
- **Usage and device data.** Sign-in times, the features you use, your device and browser, your IP address, and error logs.
- **Beta information.** Your application, whether you're accepted, your feedback, and your Premium Reward under the Beta Terms.

### 2.3 Sensitive information

We don't ask for sensitive information, such as health, racial or religious information. Please don't put it in the App. If a photo shows people or private things, tell us and we'll help you remove it.
